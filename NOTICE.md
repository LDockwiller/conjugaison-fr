  `verbs-fr.xml` and `conjugations-fr.xml`, the French verb list and
  conjugation templates of [Verbiste](https://perso.b2b2c.ca/~sarrazip/dev/verbiste.html)
  by Pierre Sarrazin (Copyright (C) 2003-2016, GPL-2.0-or-later), as
  distributed with improvements by Brett Tolbert in
  [verbecc](https://github.com/bretttolbert/verbecc).
- `BarWidget.qml` and the layout of `Panel.qml` are adapted from
  [Dictionary & Thesaurus](https://github.com/dlpwaters/omarchy-dictionary-thesaurus)
  by DLP Waters (MIT License).
